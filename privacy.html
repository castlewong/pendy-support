Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026 · Pendy for macOS

Pendy is a local app for organizing screenshots, photos, diaries, sticky notes, and to-dos. It does not require an account. Wilbur Dave does not operate a server that receives your content.

Content on your Mac

Notes and imported images are saved in Pendy’s local Application Support folder. Removing a card from the line does not delete it from the library. When you choose “Move to Trash” in the library, macOS moves that file to the Trash.

Folders and clipboard

If you choose a screenshot folder through the macOS file picker, Pendy reads new images there and saves local copies. It does not change your system screenshot settings or the original files. Clipboard image monitoring is off until you enable it. When enabled, Pendy checks for newly copied images and keeps local copies in its Clipboard folder. Taking one of those images off the line moves its copy to the Trash.

Purchases

If you buy a membership, Apple handles payment and provides Pendy with a verified purchase status to unlock styles. Pendy does not receive your payment card details. Purchases may be processed under Apple’s own privacy policy.

To-do reminders

If you set a reminder, Pendy asks macOS for notification permission and schedules a local notification. Declining permission does not prevent a to-do from being saved.

Agent status (optional)

If you select a local Agent events folder, Pendy reads its status.json file and saves the provider name, short task title and state as a local card. Pendy does not read Agent conversations or approve requests. Avoid placing secrets in the task title.

Collection and sharing

Pendy currently has no developer analytics, advertising SDK, account system, or cloud synchronization. The developer does not collect or share your notes, photos, screenshots, or clipboard images. Pendy opens Apple’s purchase services only for in-app purchases and restoration.

隐私政策

2026 年 10 月 14 日生效 · Pendy macOS 版

Pendy 是整理截图、照片、日记、便签和待办的本机应用，无需注册账号。开发者 Wilbur Dave 不运营接收你内容的服务器。

保存在本机的内容

笔记和导入的图片保存在 Pendy 的本机 Application Support 文件夹中。从绳上取下卡片不会删除资料库文件。在资料库中选择“移到废纸篓”时，macOS 会把对应文件移到废纸篓。

文件夹与剪贴板

如果你通过 macOS 文件选择器选定截图文件夹，Pendy 会读取其中新增的图片并保存本机副本；它不会更改系统截图设置或原文件。剪贴板图片监听默认关闭；你开启后，Pendy 才会检查新复制的图片，并在本机 Clipboard 文件夹保留副本。从绳上取下这类图片会将副本移到废纸篓。

购买

购买会员时，Apple 处理付款，并向 Pendy 提供已验证的购买状态以解锁样式。Pendy 不接收你的银行卡信息。购买可能受 Apple 自身隐私政策约束。

待办提醒

如果你为待办设置提醒，Pendy 会向 macOS 请求通知权限并安排本机通知。拒绝通知权限不影响保存待办。

智能体状态（可选）

如果你选择本机智能体事件文件夹，Pendy 会读取其中的 status.json，将来源、简短任务标题和状态保存为本机卡片。Pendy 不读取智能体对话，也不会代替你批准请求。请勿在任务标题中放入密码等私密信息。

数据收集与分享

当前版本没有开发者分析、广告 SDK、账号或云同步。开发者不收集或分享你的笔记、照片、截图或剪贴板图片。应用仅在内购及恢复购买时使用 Apple 的购买服务。
