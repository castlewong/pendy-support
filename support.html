Support

Pendy helps you hang screenshots, photos, diaries, notes, and to-dos on a line across your Mac desktop.

How do I keep the line visible?

Click the Pendy icon in the menu bar and turn on “Keep Line Visible”. The setting is remembered when you restart the app.

How do I add screenshots automatically?

Choose “Choose Screenshot Folder…” from the Pendy menu and select the folder where macOS saves your screenshots. Pendy makes its own local copy of each new image.

Where did my cards go?

Removing a card from the line leaves imported photos and notes in the Pendy Library. Open the library from the app or menu bar, then click Hang to bring an item back.

How do I restore a membership?

Open Settings with ⌘, and select Membership, then choose “Restore Purchases”. Annual subscriptions can be managed in Apple’s subscription settings.

Why can’t I hang a third item?

The free version holds two items on the line. Anything you write or import remains in the library when the line is full. Membership unlocks more hanging space.

Privacy Policy

帮助与支持

Pendy 帮你把截图、照片、日记、便签和待办挂在 Mac 桌面的晾衣绳上。

如何让晾衣绳常驻？

点击菜单栏的 Pendy 图标，开启“常驻挂衣绳”。重启应用后设置仍会保留。

如何自动挂上截图？

从 Pendy 菜单中选择“选择截图文件夹…”，选定 macOS 存放截图的文件夹。Pendy 会为新图片保存独立的本机副本。

卡片取下后在哪里？

从绳上取下卡片后，导入的照片和笔记仍在 Pendy 资料库。打开资料库，点击“挂上”即可重新放回绳上。

如何恢复会员？

按 ⌘, 打开“设置”，进入“会员”，选择“恢复购买”。年费订阅可在 Apple 订阅设置中管理。

为什么挂不上第 3 件？

免费版的绳上最多同时挂 2 件。绳子满了，写下或导入的内容仍会留在资料库；会员可解锁更多悬挂位置。
